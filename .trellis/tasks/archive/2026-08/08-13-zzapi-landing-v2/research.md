# Research and V1 Review

## Findings

- V1 ties Entrance lifetime to `isLoaded`; fast responses truncate it and slow
  responses create an indefinite loader.
- Entrance and Hero are mutually exclusive, so the page visibly restarts.
- The public header appears during the supposed empty-space frame.
- V1 never displays `zzapi`; it uses `API` and `systemName || 'New API'`.
- Its signature motion is a small repeating sweep over a 44px hero icon.
- Square grid, circular orbit, theme-preset primary color, and independent glows
  do not express the official logo's rounded diagonal geometry.
- Gateway model hover does not affect a route or core response.

## Brand Extraction

Keep the supplied 256x256 PNG unchanged. Representative colors are deep
`#1549F4`, primary `#5C8EFF`, flow `#7DAAFF`, light `#AED1FF`, and highlight
`#E0F4FF`. Its rounded rising-Z shape suggests one controlled 45-degree axis,
soft directional light, and precise data flow.

## Reference Principles

The reference is used only to study why an entrance has impact: one dominant
event, tight timing, clear hierarchy, spatial continuity, and brand reveal. The
zzapi storyboard is independently derived from its official mark and gateway.
